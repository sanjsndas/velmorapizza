Privacy & Data

Your privacy matters to us. This policy outlines how Velmora Pizza Reverie collects, uses, and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to improve your experience. This includes:

  • Analytics: anonymized usage data via cookies (see our Cookie Policy)
  • Newsletter: email address if you opt-in to our monthly pizza digest
  • Comments: name, email, and IP address (only if you leave a comment on articles)

We never sell your data to third parties. Ever.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

PurposeLegal basisData used
Improving contentLegitimate interestAnonymized analytics
Newsletter deliveryConsentEmail address
Comment moderationLegitimate interestName, email, IP

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for functionality and optional cookies for analytics. You can manage your preferences in our Cookie Policy.

🔒 Your rights: You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. Contact us at privacy@velmorapizza.com.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined. Analytics data is stored for 26 months. Newsletter subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics – anonymized traffic analysis (opt-out available)
  • Mailchimp – for newsletter distribution (your email is stored on their secure servers)
  • Disqus – for comment management (if enabled)

Each service has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

6. Security

We take security seriously. Our site uses HTTPS encryption, and we regularly audit our systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our site or via email.

📬 Questions? Reach out to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@velmorapizza.com
